Privacy Policy for Meteor Strike

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Meteor Strike is published by StrikingGames. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you use the game, what the game stores locally on your device, and how third-party services such as Google AdMob, Google Analytics for Firebase and Google Play Games Services may handle data.

Meteor Strike does not require you to create an account with StrikingGames. The game can be played without signing in. Google Play Games is optional and is used only for the leaderboards.

1. Information We Store Locally on Your Device

Meteor Strike stores a small amount of gameplay and settings data locally on your device, such as:

  • Your best score, your best of the day and your best in each day's Daily Strike
  • Your daily play streak and your progress on the day's missions
  • Free revives you have earned, and when the game last asked about reminders
  • Your music, sound effects, and haptics preferences
  • Temporary ad and privacy-related state needed for the app to function correctly

This local data is used only to operate the game experience on your device. It is not sent to a StrikingGames server because Meteor Strike does not currently use its own game backend.

2. Advertising and Consent

Meteor Strike uses Google AdMob to display ads, including rewarded ads and banner ads. AdMob and related Google technologies may process information such as device identifiers, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, app interactions, diagnostics, and ad performance information.

The game also uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request or manage consent when required by applicable law or by Google's policies. A privacy or consent message is not shown to every player by default. It is shown only when required for the player, region, and ad configuration in effect at that time.

When available, you can access privacy choices inside the game through the in-app privacy entry point.

3. Google Play Games Leaderboards

Meteor Strike has Google Play Games leaderboards on Android: the main leaderboard and the Daily Strike leaderboard. If your device is signed in to Google Play Games, the game may sign in automatically and submit your scores; you can also sign in from the leaderboard screen. Google may process information associated with your Play Games account and leaderboard activity, such as your player profile, player identifier, and submitted scores, and other players can see your Play Games name, avatar and scores on the leaderboards.

You do not need Google Play Games to play. You can manage your Play Games profile and its visibility in the Google Play Games settings on your device.

4. Analytics

Meteor Strike uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is played, so that it can be improved, and to measure how well its advertising campaigns bring in players. It collects:

  • An app-instance identifier assigned to your installation of the game
  • Your device's advertising ID
  • Approximate location derived from your device's masked IP address
  • In-game events, such as starting and ending a run, scores and run lengths, revives, missions completed, sharing a score, and opening the game from a reminder
  • Sessions, and basic information about your device and the app version

Where consent is required, Analytics follows the choices you make in the game's privacy and consent message. You can reset your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in your device's settings. Analytics data is sent to Google encrypted in transit and is handled under Google's privacy policy.

5. Reminders

If you allow notifications, Meteor Strike schedules reminders on your device when you leave the game: at most one a day, only during the day, and none after two weeks without playing. They are created on your device, not sent from a server. You can turn them off at any time in your device's notification settings for Meteor Strike.

6. Sharing Your Score

When you tap the share button, the game creates a picture of your score on your device and opens Android's share sheet. The picture and message go only to the app and people you choose to share them with; StrikingGames does not receive them.

7. Third-Party Services

Meteor Strike currently uses the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob for advertising
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) for ad consent and privacy messaging
  • Google Analytics for Firebase for analytics
  • Google Play Games Services for the leaderboards

Google's Privacy Policy is available here:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

8. How We Use Information

Information processed through the game and its service providers may be used to:

  • Run core gameplay features and save local preferences
  • Show ads, including rewarded ads
  • Support consent and privacy controls where required
  • Submit and display leaderboard scores
  • Understand how the game is played and improve it
  • Measure the performance of our advertising campaigns
  • Send reminders, if you allow notifications
  • Maintain service integrity, prevent fraud, and diagnose technical issues

9. Data Sharing

StrikingGames does not sell your personal information directly. However, data may be processed by the third-party providers listed above as part of advertising, consent management, analytics, and leaderboard functionality.

10. Data Retention

Data stored locally on your device generally remains until you clear app data or uninstall the app. Data processed by third-party providers is subject to their own retention practices and policies.

11. Children's Privacy

Meteor Strike is not intended to knowingly collect personal information directly from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to a third-party service through the app in a way that should be addressed, please contact us and we will review the issue.

12. Security

We take reasonable steps to use reputable service providers and standard platform protections. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and third-party services operate under their own security practices.

13. External Links

The app or this page may contain links to external sites operated by third parties. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of those third-party sites.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make changes, we will post the updated version on this page and revise the date at the top.
